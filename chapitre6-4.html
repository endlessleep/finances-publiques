Chapter 6: The Economics of Old-Age Pensions

The Ultimate Choice: Which System Makes You Richer?

The most important question in pension economics is simple: which system gives you a better mathematical profit for your long life?

The PAYG Pipe

$r_{PAYG} = n + g$

Pop. Growth + Salary Growth

Current Reality in Europe: Babies are rare ($n \approx 0.1\%$), salaries grow slowly ($g \approx 1.0\%$).

→ Total Profit: ONLY $\approx 1\text{--}1.5\%$

The Stock Market (Funded)

$r_{funded} = r$

Stock Market Magic

Historical Stock Profit: ≈ 4–7%

(But remember, this comes with pure terrifying risk of losing everything in a crash).

💡 The Answer: Simply look to see if the biology number ($n + g$) is higher or lower than the stock casino ($r$)! This is the legendary "Aaron's Rule".

The War Between Individuals and the State

There is a furious battle between what you secretly want and what the country can actually survive:

What You Want (Micro)

Every single citizen wants massive pension checks and zero taxes. You just want the best personal profit possible from the system.

What The Country Needs (Macro)

The President knows that if he gives you massive checks and charges you no taxes, the national debt will explode and the country will go completely bankrupt.

The President's Delicate Balance: The government constantly changes the math. For example, in the Czech Republic, the government takes 28% of your youth salary, and later gives you about 40–45% of it back when you are old. They also heavily rig the math to be kind — poor cleaners get a much better deal than millionaire bankers.

The Brutal Reality: Czech Republic Math

Does the government pension actually give you a good profit? Let's check the real math from the Czech Republic:

True Profit for Czech Citizens (2015 Data):

Wait, Should We Just Use the Stock Market?

  • The Czech stock market magically generated roughly a 1.6% profit (after inflation) over 30 years. Much better than the −1.4% above!
  • The giant American stock market (S&P 500) generated a stunning 8.0% return over the last 25 years.
  • The Global stock market makes about 11.5% profit recently.
  • THE DANGER: If a Czech person invests in America, they face massive currency risk. If the Czech Crown suddenly gets super strong against the Dollar, your American profits are instantly deleted.

Aaron's Rule (1966): The Golden Math Equation

In 1966, a genius named Henry Aaron solved the puzzle in an American journal. He proved exactly when the PAYG pipe is better than the stock market casino.

The Final Formula:

PAYG makes everyone happier ONLY if: $\quad n + g > r$

If $n + g > r$

The "Biology Profit" (lots of babies and huge salary growth) is magically stronger than the stock market. You should definitely use the PAYG state pension. Everyone gets richer!

If $n + g = r$

A pure tie. It literally doesn't matter which system you use, they pay you the exact same amount. This is a rare, perfectly balanced universe.

If $n + g < r$

The Stock Market is vastly superior. If the government forces you to use PAYG, they are essentially robbing you of huge stock market profits. You are losing money.

The Shocking Truth:

For Italy and Japan, biology is literally negative! Their PAYG systems are terrible math deals. This proves exactly why they were forced to invent fake private accounts (NDC) or deeply respect the stock market.

For the USA, biology technically wins (lots of immigration). BUT, they are still spending too much, proving that even if the math is in your favor, a greedy President can still bankrupt the country.

How To Save a Doomed Country

If you are the President of a dying European country where the Aaron math completely fails, what are your only 4 survival options?

01

Force People to Work Until 70

Because humans live longer, just legally force them to retire much later. You don't have to slash the monthly checks, you just make them wait longer. It is painful, but mathematically the easiest trick to perform.

02

The NDC Trick

Use the clever system from Sweden and Italy. Make citizens believe they possess a magical private account, but actually just lower their checks automatically whenever the economy performs badly. It solves the math without making voters furiously angry.

03

The Three-Layer Cake

Don't bet on one horse. Build a mixed machine: (1) A small PAYG state system so poor people don't starve, (2) forced private stock market accounts, and (3) completely optional extra savings. A mix protects you from every risk.

04

The Giant Emergency Fund

Keep the state system, but start hoarding extra tax money into a colossal, secret government stock market account (Norway does this perfectly). Use that secret wealth later to pay the grandparents without needing the young.

Chapter 6 — Fast Facts

  1. Biology runs the world.

    The Old-Age Dependency Ratio (OADR) is the only number that matters. The youth burden in Europe will double to 57% by 2070. Unreformed systems will instantly die.

  2. PAYG makes exactly $n + g$.

    The state system isn't magic. Your profit relies entirely on a nation having many babies and fast salary growth.

  3. Private Banks make exactly $r$.

    Private accounts use the powerful stock market to make you rich, but they contain hidden, terrifying risks of crashes and bad luck.

  4. Aaron holds the crown.

    Only use the state system if $n + g > r$. Because Europe and Japan have stopped having babies, this rule has crashed, forcing them to look urgently to stock market solutions.

  5. Don't Be Stupid: Mix Them.

    No single plan is perfect. The smartest countries combine a kind state system with a powerful stock market system to build a bullet-proof three-layer cake.

Chapter 6.4 — Aaron's Rule:

  • Aaron's Equation: The state system only works perfectly if $n + g > r$. If it doesn't, you are literally losing money compared to just investing in stocks.
  • In today's world, Italy and Japan completely fail this math test. The USA barely passes it. Therefore, relying 100% on the state is becoming mathematically dangerous everywhere.
  • Even in the Czech system, the math proves that average workers lose money (their taxes go to the poor).
  • The only way to survive is a multi-pillar system — where the country actively uses BOTH the state and private stock accounts so nothing can completely destroy your retirement.
