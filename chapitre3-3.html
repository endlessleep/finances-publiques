3.3 — Smart Expenditure Management

Chapter 3: Fiscal-Monetary Policy Nexus

Options for Fixing the Budget

University textbooks say that government debt is perfectly fixed by using "passive" magic taxes that don't change human behavior. But this is a purely mathematical dream — these magic taxes don't exist in real life.

Real-world studies prove that successfully fixing a broken budget almost always requires a painful combination of raising real taxes and cutting government spending. When it comes to spending cuts, governments choose between two completely different methods:

❌ Not Very Smart

Blind Blanket Cuts

Every single government department loses the exact same 10% of its budget. This is very easy to do and looks "fair" to voters — but it completely crushes the most hardworking and efficient departments and almost always kills the most important future investments (like building schools). It's a brutish, blind method that refuses to separate brilliant programs from total garbage spending.

✅ Smart

Targeted, Precision Cuts

Every spending program is judged individually on its own merit. Terrible programs are murdered; brilliant programs are protected or even rewarded with more cash. This requires massive amounts of hard analysis but delivers vastly superior results. Two major modern tools make this dream possible: performance budgeting and spending reviews.

Traditional vs. Performance Budgeting

Old Dinosaur Method

📋 Traditional Budgeting

  • Obsesses over the starting amount of money but completely ignores whether the money actually accomplished anything.
  • Always relies on lazy copy-pasting ("just give them what they got last year plus 2%").
  • Impossible to figure out if the money was wasted, impossible to measure efficiency, and impossible to audit properly.
  • The national budget becomes nothing more than a dusty accountant's diary instead of a powerful steering wheel for improving the country.
  • Utterly fails to link the money to important national goals or real-life success.
Modern Intelligent Method

🎯 Performance Budgeting

  • A fresh, highly organized philosophy for public money.
  • Demands to know not just the amount of cash burned, but strictly links it to the actual real-world impact achieved.
  • Ties every dollar directly to a specific winning goal of a government policy.
  • Dramatically forces efficiency, honesty, and blame-shifting removal across public offices.
  • Forces all money to be legally organized by the exact ultimate purpose it serves.
💡 OECD ranking reality check: Czechia is a massive failure at performance budgeting compared to other rich OECD countries (Source: OECD, 2023). This is sad — but it also proves there is gigantic room for easy improvements. They can unlock huge efficiency wins and cleanly harvest billions in wasted money. A baby step was attempted during the 2026 budget plans ("Annex H"), but a total revolution is still needed.

The Planned Dream

🔄 The Goal State

  • Hardwire the budget directly to national plans.
  • Force all money decisions to be justified by performance data.

The Proposed Fix (CVF, 2025)

The Three Rules

To fix the nation's wallet, the reform must stand on 3 heavy pillars:

  1. Goal-driven DNA: Public money, especially the main state budget, must be categorized purely by what the policy actually aims to fix; the money files must mirror these goals perfectly.
  2. Judgment day: There must be a brutal process for inventing and grading the success of every single dollar spent and earned.
  3. Naked transparency: Budget documents and end-of-year reports must showcase where the money went using completely goal-focused language.
💰 The Jackpot: If done right, this reform promises pure savings of around 3% of all government spending (roughly CZK 100 billion) and makes a further 10% (roughly CZK 350 billion) dramatically more effective. Source: CVF (2025).

Who is Responsible? (The Stakeholders)

The Player Their Job
🏦 Ministry of Finance
  • Designs the master rulebook for how performance budgeting works.
  • Builds the exact forms and spreadsheets that agencies must fill out to prove their worth.
  • Forces all other ministries to play by the exact same rules.
  • Inspects what the spending agencies actually manage to produce.
  • Trains government workers and upgrades the computer software smoothly.
🏛️ Spending Entities (Ministries, Agencies)
  • Negotiate with the Ministry of Finance to define their programs, missions, and scorecards.
  • Do the real, heavy daily work: tracking data, writing reports.
  • Invent smart scorecards that truly measure what the leading politicians desire.
  • Force the most powerful boss (the Minister) to personally sign off and swear that the performance data is honest.
🏛️ Parliament (The Politicians)
  • Act as the ultimate police and judge of the government.
  • Analyze and approve the final money numbers.
  • Study the performance scores and move money away from failing programs toward winning ones.
  • Drag ministers into committee hearings to yell at them if their performance data is garbage.
  • Special committees review the performance of their specific areas (like healthcare or defense).
🔍 Audit Offices (The Inspectors)
  • Check if the entire performance game is structured fairly and logically.
  • Hunt down lies to ensure that the reported successes are mathematically true and not just fake news.
  • Guarantee that everyone perfectly follows the law.

Spending Reviews

The Disease

📋 Current Tragedy

  • The national budget is mostly decided by lazy copy-pasting changes (usually raising last year's number), instead of mathematically proving what is actually needed.
  • When crises hit and the country needs to save money, blind blanket cuts destroy the most useful ministries and brutally murder vital investments like new infrastructure.
  • There is an enormous wall of ignorance hiding the truth between the Ministry of Finance and the other ministries when judging what cash is desperately required.
The Cure

🔍 Spending Reviews

  • A ruthless, professional investigation of spending judged totally against policy goals.
  • Secures vastly higher value for the taxpayer's money.
  • Forces a hard data-driven debate about national priorities instead of political shouting.
  • Brings blinding transparency and terrifying pressure on lazy public workers to be efficient.
  • Dramatically upgrades budget forecasting and uncovers massive hidden savings opportunities.
⚠️ The painful truth for Czechia: Only 3 experimental spending reviews have ever been attempted in Czechia recently, and there is absolutely no plan to make it a mandatory, normal habit. International gold standards declare that at least 50% of all national spending should pass through this brutal review process every 4 years.

Summary & Conclusions

Key takeaways from Chapter 3:

  • Fiscal dominance is a plague that destroys price stability.
  • Stopping it requires indestructible institutional laws — independent central banks with a singular mission for price stability and an ironclad rule against helping the state.
  • The dynamic debt math proves that the fight between interest rates ($r$) and economy growth ($g$) decides if debt explodes.
  • Four policy setups exist; the one perfect setup is an aggressive Central Bank paired with a helpful, passive Government.
  • Czechia thankfully does not suffer from fiscal dominance, but its long-term financial blood tests suggest action is needed soon.
  • A painful cocktail of tax increases and smart spending cuts will be necessary.
  • Czechia is embarrassingly behind in performance budgeting and spending reviews — which presents a massive, shiny opportunity to harvest huge savings from lazy government sectors.
  • Performance budgeting chains money directly to victories and real-world results instead of just lazily counting dollars.
  • Spending reviews ruthlessly verify taxpayer value using cold, hard data.
